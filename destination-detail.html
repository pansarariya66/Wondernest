Maldives Overwater Lagoon

5 Days / 4 Nights

Tour Overview

Escape to a private island sanctuary in the South Male Atoll. Stay in an iconic overwater villa with direct lagoon access, enjoy guided snorkeling among vibrant sea turtles, and unwind with sunset yacht cruises and gourmet beachfront dining.

Day-Wise Itinerary

Arrive at Velana International Airport where our representative welcomes you for a scenic 30-minute luxury speedboat transfer. Check into your private overwater bungalow and enjoy a welcome tropical dinner.

Morning marine biologist-led snorkeling tour across Banana Reef. Afternoon private gourmet lunch on a secluded white-sand island surrounded by crystal blue waters.

Relax with a 60-minute overwater Ayurvedic couple's massage followed by a private sunset catamaran cruise spotting spinner dolphins.

Enjoy complimentary kayaking and paddleboarding in the morning before your afternoon speedboat transfer back to Male Airport.
